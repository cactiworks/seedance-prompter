# Seedream 5.0 Pro (ByteDance, via KIE) — card

Status: official BytePlus docs for 5.0 Pro (parameters, interactive editing) plus the general Seedream
4.0–4.5 prompting guide, which the 5.0 tutorial says applies; #8–15 on the arenas, #4 in multi-image edit.

## What it is good at
- Dense infographics and UI, layout structure, realistic skin detail [vendor claim]; a menu with prices and
  book spines rendered without errors where Nano Banana Pro failed [single].
- Combining several references: multi-image edit #4, portraits in multi-image edit #4 [arena]. Higgsfield
  routes character sheets and face-preserving edits to it [official, Higgsfield skill].
- Text in 16 languages including Russian [official].

## Where it fails
- Small text and consistency in pixel-level edits [vendor admits].
- Resolution tops out at 2K (via KIE: 1K / 2K); slower than Nano Banana [single].
- "Plastic" skin was reported — add imperfection words (pores, uneven skin, film grain) [single].

## How to write the prompt
- A coherent natural sentence: subject + action + environment, then style and details. Concise and precise
  beats stacked ornate adjectives [official 4.x guide].
- Text that must appear goes in double quotes [official].
- When references are given, say what each gives: "Replace the subject in image 1 with the subject from
  image 2", "Apply the style of image 2 to image 1", "Dress the character in image 1 with the outfit from
  image 2" [official]. Say explicitly what must stay unchanged [official].
- Up to ~600 words is accepted; shorter is better [official].
- Sheets and grids: describe the layout ("a character sheet on a white background: front, side, three-quarter
  and full-body views of the same person") — no official sheet template exists [not found].

## Parameters
- resolution: 1K, 2K. aspectRatio: 9:16, 3:4, 2:3, 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 21:9. Up to 10 reference images
  (each extra image after the first costs +0.5 credits).
